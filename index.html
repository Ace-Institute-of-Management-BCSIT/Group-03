Our Features

Purpose-built for Nepali nonprofits and social organizations; one platform for all stakeholders.

Donation

Accept money & item donations, track history, generate receipts and transparency reports.

Volunteer

Register volunteers, post opportunities, schedule shifts, and track hours and participation in real time.

Event and Needs

Create and manage social events and campaigns. Post needs of an organization.

Analytics

Dashboards showing donation trends, volunteer statistics, and campaign performance for all roles.
