Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from our community.

How do I donate to a specific cause?
Visit the Donate page, select a cause, choose your amount and donation type, then click Donate Now. You'll receive a receipt via email.
Is my donation secure?
Yes. All transactions are encrypted and processed through secure payment gateways. CareConnect has a 98% transparency score, and all donation flows are fully auditable.
How do I register as a volunteer?
Go to the Volunteer page, fill in the quick registration form, and select your skills and availability. Once registered, you can apply for opportunities directly.
How long does NGO verification take?
NGO verification typically takes 24–48 working hours. Our team reviews your registration number, contact details, and organization description before issuing the verified badge.
Can I donate physical items instead of money?
Yes! On the Donate page, select "Items / Goods" as your donation type. You'll arrange a meetup time and location with the NGO to hand over your donated goods.
Is CareConnect free to use?
CareConnect is completely free for donors and volunteers. NGOs can register and use core features at no cost. Premium analytics and tools for larger organizations are available on request.
How can I track my past donations?
Once you sign in to your donor account, your full donation history, receipts, and impact reports are available in your personal dashboard.
Can I volunteer from outside Nepal?
Yes! Many NGOs on CareConnect accept remote volunteers for tasks like digital marketing, content creation, translation, and online tutoring.
