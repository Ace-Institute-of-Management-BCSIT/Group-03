CareConnect connects donors, volunteers, and verified NGOs across Nepal — so you can see exactly where your support lands.

What is CareConnect?

CareConnect is a platform built for Nepal's giving community. We verify every NGO before they can list a cause, log every donation against a real campaign goal, and give volunteers a direct line to organizations that need their skills — replacing scattered messages and paper receipts with one transparent record.

Built for transparency

Four things that set CareConnect apart from a typical donation page.

Verified NGOs only

Every organization is reviewed and approved before they can publish a cause or accept volunteers.

Live progress tracking

Each cause shows funds raised against its goal in real time, so you always know what your gift achieved.

Cash or item donations

Give money directly, or arrange a meetup to hand over goods like clothes, books, or food.

Volunteer matching

Browse open opportunities by district and skill, and apply directly to the NGO running them.
